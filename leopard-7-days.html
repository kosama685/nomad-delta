Days 1 - 2

Moremi Game Reserve

Depart Maun and journey into the heart of Moremi. Expect dense mopane woodlands and spectacular floodplains. Afternoon game drives offer excellent chances to spot wild dog and, true to the package name, elusive leopards.

Days 3 - 4

Khwai Concession

Move camp to the Khwai River area. This region is famous for its high concentration of elephants and predators. Experience the Delta intimately from a traditional dugout canoe (mokoro) as you glide through papyrus-lined channels.

Days 5 - 6

Savuti Marsh

Travel north to the starkly beautiful Savuti region. Known for its raw, dramatic landscapes and the mysterious Savuti Channel. Engage in a thrilling guided walking safari to read tracks and understand the micro-ecosystems.

Return to Maun
