Experience the seasonal floods, incredible wildlife abundance, and the silent, profound beauty of a mokoro journey through the winding papyrus channels.

A World Shaped by Water

The Okavango Delta is a unique pulse of life in the heart of the Kalahari Desert. Unlike most deltas, its waters never reach the sea, instead evaporating and infiltrating into the sands, creating a fluctuating labyrinth of lagoons, channels, and islands.

Moremi Game Reserve, protecting the central and eastern areas of the Delta, offers some of the most spectacular game viewing in Africa. Here, the transition from wetland to dry woodland provides diverse habitats for an astonishing array of species.
