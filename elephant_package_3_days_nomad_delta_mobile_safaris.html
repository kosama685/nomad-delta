Arrival & Sunset Cruise

Arrive in Kasane and transfer to your riverside lodge. In the afternoon, embark on a signature Chobe River boat cruise. This is prime time for viewing elephants crossing the river and hippos relaxing in the shallows as the sun sets.

02
directions_car

Full Day Game Drives

A full day dedicated to exploring the Chobe riverfront in a customized 4x4 safari vehicle. Known for its massive elephant populations, the park also offers excellent chances to spot lions, leopards, and large herds of buffalo. Enjoy a picnic lunch in the wild.

03
wb_twilight

Sunrise Safari & Departure

An early wake-up call for a final morning game drive as the bush awakens. Watch predators finishing their nightly hunts before returning to the lodge for a hearty breakfast. Transfer to Kasane airport for your onward journey.
