Day-by-Day Expedition

1-2

Maun & Moremi Game Reserve

Arrival in Maun, the gateway to the Okavango. Meet your expert guide and transfer to your first mobile camp in the renowned Moremi Game Reserve. The next two days are dedicated to extensive game drives exploring the rich mopane woodlands and floodplains, searching for leopard, wild dog, and large elephant herds.

airport_shuttle Game Drives photo_camera Photography
3-4

Deep Okavango Delta

Transition from land to water as we move deeper into the Delta. Experience the profound silence of the bush gliding in a traditional Mokoro (dugout canoe) through papyrus-lined channels. We will also conduct guided walking safaris on pristine islands, focusing on tracking, birdlife, and the intricate micro-ecosystems often missed from a vehicle.

rowing Mokoro Excursions directions_walk Walking Safaris
5-6

Savuti Predator Capital

Journey north into the arid and dramatic landscapes of Savuti, renowned for its dense predator populations. Spend these days tracking the famous Savuti lion prides and large clans of spotted hyenas. The contrasting scenery of dead acacia trees against the Savuti marsh provides a spectacular backdrop for dramatic wildlife encounters.

7-8

Chobe National Park

Arrive at the Chobe Riverfront, famous for hosting the largest concentration of elephants in Africa. Enjoy extensive game drives along the riverbanks and a spectacular afternoon private boat cruise, offering incredible photographic opportunities of elephants bathing and hippos podding in the golden light.

directions_boat Boat Cruise
9-10

Leisure & Departure

Enjoy a final early morning safari in Chobe before a leisurely breakfast in camp. Transfer to Kasane or Victoria Falls for your onward journey, carrying the dust of the Delta and memories of a lifetime.
